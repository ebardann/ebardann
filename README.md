# Hi, I'm Emilio 👋

**Applied mathematics undergraduate** working where geometry, algebra, and learning systems meet.
I'm interested in *why* neural networks work — what structure they discover, how symmetry constrains them, and what that tells us about the data they model.

> Currently: writing my bachelor's thesis on the mechanistic interpretability of AlphaFold, and preparing for graduate study in interpretability.

## 🔬 Research interests

- **Mechanistic interpretability** — circuits, superposition (Grassmannian packing, Welch bounds, compressed sensing), sparse dictionaries, and formal concept analysis over learned features
- **Geometric deep learning** — SE(3)/SO(3)-equivariant architectures, Lie group representations (Mackey machine, Clebsch–Gordan, Wigner-D)
- **Protein structure ML** — AlphaFold 2/3, RoseTTAFold, diffusion on SE(3) frames
- **Applied algebraic topology** — persistent homology and topological data analysis on time series

## 🧪 Current & recent work

### Interpreting AlphaFold's Pairformer *(bachelor's thesis, in progress)*
Does AlphaFold represent intrinsic physicochemical properties (e.g. hydrophobicity) that it then translates into geometry? Does evolutionary signal propagate into its predictions in identifiable ways? Rather than the diffusion module, this work targets the **Pairformer** and its triangular attention, at the level of heads and circuits.

### Persistent homology from first principles → TDA-EEG pipeline
A mathematics paper building persistent homology from the ground up — simplicial homology over 𝔽₂, Vietoris–Rips and Čech filtrations and their interleaving, the structure theorem for persistence modules — and applying it to EEG via Takens delay embeddings, with Wasserstein distance between diagrams as a dynamical signal.

### Information decay in the Lorenz attractor
Estimating the mutual information decay $I(t) = I(x(0);\, x(t))$ with the KSG estimator, a burstiness measure $\sigma^2_{\dot I}$, parameter sweeps over $\rho$, cryptographic interpretations, and an interactive visualization.

### Optimization
An interior-point LP solver in MATLAB, alongside duality work on $L^1$ regression.
